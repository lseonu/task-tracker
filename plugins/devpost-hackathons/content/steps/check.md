# Run your final check

Review completeness, security, and submission readiness.

Codex will summarize whether you are ready, close, or not ready to submit.

This step includes the local security scan result, with redacted findings only.

If the result is ready, you have two ways to finish:

- **Submit from Codex** — say "yes, submit" when prompted and Codex submits your project to Devpost through the official Devpost connection (you'll be shown exactly what will be submitted first, and you may need to sign in to Devpost).
- **Submit in the browser** — open the official Devpost submission page, copy the final material from `devpost-submission.md`, add the repository link, demo link, screenshots, and demo video, and submit the official form before the deadline.

Nothing is ever submitted without your explicit confirmation.

If the result is close or not ready, fix the listed blockers first and run `$submission` again.
